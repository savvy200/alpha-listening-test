# 10 Hz Listening Test · 欢迎加入测试

**[🌐 公开试听网站 / Open Listening Website](https://savvy200.github.io/alpha-listening-test/)** · **[💬 试听反馈 / Listening Feedback](https://github.com/savvy200/alpha-listening-test/issues/new?template=audio-feedback.yml)**

**中文：** 欢迎免费试听两段各约5分钟的原创10 Hz双耳节拍音乐。我们希望了解不同的音乐风格对主观放松感、舒适度和聆听体验有何影响。请使用立体声耳机，从较低音量开始。

**English:** Welcome to a voluntary listening experience featuring two original, approximately five-minute 10 Hz binaural-beat music tracks. We are interested in subjective relaxation, comfort, and sound-quality feedback. Use stereo headphones and start at a low volume.

> **健康与科研说明 / Health & research notice:** 本项目是探索性自愿试听，不是临床试验、医疗器械或医学治疗。现有文献不能证明两段音频可治疗ALS（渐冻症）、脑卒中或其他疾病，也不能证明延缓病程。**This is a voluntary listening experience, not a clinical trial, medical device, or medical treatment. Neither recording is proven to treat ALS, stroke, or any other disease or to slow disease progression.**

## ⚠️ 安全须知 / Listening safety

**中文：** 请使用立体声耳机，低音量开始；如出现头痛、耳鸣、眩晕或其他不适应停止。聆听不得掩盖医疗设备警报或照护人员指令。不要以此代替医疗及康复治疗。

**English:** Use stereo headphones and begin at a low volume. Stop if you experience headache, ringing in the ears, dizziness, or other discomfort. Never mask medical-device alarms or caregiver instructions, or replace medical or rehabilitation care with these recordings.
